# Day 3 EDA Insights

## Key Findings

1. Mutual fund NAVs showed consistent long-term growth across most schemes.
2. SBI Mutual Fund maintained the highest AUM among major AMCs.
3. SIP inflows increased steadily throughout the analysis period.
4. Equity categories attracted the highest investor inflows.
5. Investor participation expanded significantly across regions.
6. Folio count increased from 13.26 Cr to 26.12 Cr between 2022 and 2025.
7. Fund returns within similar categories showed high correlation.
8. Equity funds displayed stronger long-term performance than debt-oriented schemes.
9. Investment activity was concentrated among a few major states.
10. A small number of fund houses controlled a significant share of industry assets.
